# Failure Modes — finplan-content
# How the content work itself fails, and the corrective baked into the skill.

| # | Failure mode | What it looks like | Corrective (enforced in SKILL.md) |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | False rigor in the words | Copy calls an estimate a "guarantee," a sampled outcome "what you will have," or leaves an optimistic simplification (income at full CPI, pre-tax) unstated so it reads as conservative. | Every projection reads "estimate." Every engine simplification is caveated in plain language where the number appears (rows 1, 9). Check the label against what the math actually does (glossary). |
| F2 | Invented authority | A statistic or methodology claim asserted with no source, or "the research shows…" with nothing behind it. | researching-with-confidence on every factual claim; cite the primary source (Bengen, Trinity, SSA, IRS, the named book) or cut it. Never invent a citation. |
| F3 | Scope creep into the math | "Clarifying" the copy quietly changes the asserted number, rate, or treatment. | Reword for clarity only. The claim CFP ratified is the claim; if clarity needs the assertion to change, hand back to CFP — do not move a number. |
| F4 | AI voice | Banned constructions ("This isn't X — it's Y"), "genuinely/honestly," em-dash pile-ups, tricolons — copy that reads machine-made and erodes trust. | preston-writing is mandatory for any prose. Scan against the banned list before placing copy. |
| F5 | Jargon mistaken for explanation | A term is "explained" with another term ("safe withdrawal rate = your SWR"), or the helper restates the label without adding the "why." | The "why" answers what it is AND why it matters to the plan, in words a first-timer who has read zero finance books understands. Gloss every term. |
| F6 | Judging the user | Copy implies a category is "too much," names a shortfall "failing," or shames a spending choice. | Sethi framing: no category is bad. "Gap to close," not "behind." Frame around the user's values and the next move. |
| F7 | Clear in the editor, broken on screen | Helper text reads fine in the file but overflows the card, buries the input, or wraps oddly in context. | Read the copy in Claude_Preview, in place. Length and placement are part of clarity. |
