# Failure Modes — finplan-director
# How the briefing itself fails, and the corrective baked into the skill.

| # | Failure mode | What it looks like | Corrective (enforced in SKILL.md) |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Inflated scorecard | An axis is reported "up" without an audit or row to back it. | Every scorecard delta links to an audit, an errors.md row, or a metric. Directional and honest, never optimistic. |
| F2 | Done with an open Critical | The briefing calls the work shippable while a Critical is unresolved. | Definition-of-Done is per-item; nothing is "done" with an open Critical. The Director reports status; Preston ships. |
| F3 | Buried recurrence | A re-opened errors.md row is noted in passing instead of leading. | A recurrence is a protocol failure and leads the briefing — it is the most important thing Preston needs to know. |
| F4 | Aspiration as fact | "What moved" lists intentions, not shipped commits. | Ground every "moved" claim in a commit or an audit; what's planned goes under decision asks, not under what moved. |
| F5 | No decision | The briefing summarizes but never frames the call Preston must make. | The decision asks section is the point: explicit options + a recommendation for each open call. |
| F6 | Doing the work | The Director "fixes" something, re-audits, or commits while compiling. | Synthesize only. Route any needed work back to the owning agent; Preston holds ship authority. |
| F7 | AI voice | Forbidden constructions or AI markers in a briefing meant to read as Preston's. | Write in Preston's voice: BLUF, active, evidence-grounded; no "This isn't X — it's Y," no "genuinely." |
